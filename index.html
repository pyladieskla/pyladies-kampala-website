PyLadies Kampala — 2026 Open Source Summit

6th Annual Open Source Summit
200 Participants Expected
15 Mentors · 13:1 Ratio
100% Python-Focused
Free To Attend

Objective

1
Hands-On Sessions

Through hands-on sessions, participants will strengthen their understanding of Python, web development, and open-source collaboration while gaining the confidence to build and contribute to real-world projects.

2
Pathways & Connections

The summit will also provide participants with opportunities to connect with experienced developers, mentors, and other members of the Python community. Through these connections, we aim to expose participants to pathways such as open-source contributions, internships, mentorship programs, and other opportunities within the technology ecosystem.

3
Local Community

Beyond the technical training, the summit aims to strengthen the local community of women in technology by creating a space where participants can learn, collaborate, share experiences, and build lasting connections. By bringing together women from different backgrounds and levels of experience, we hope to encourage greater participation in the Python and open-source communities and inspire more women to pursue careers and leadership opportunities in technology.

4
Beyond the Event

Ultimately, the summit will contribute to building a more inclusive, skilled, and connected Python community in Uganda, while creating opportunities for participants to continue learning and contributing beyond the event.

Beneficiaries

The main beneficiaries will be women and other underrepresented people in tech, including students, early-career developers, and aspiring programmers. We expect to reach around 200 participants from the PyLadies Kampala community and other local tech communities. We will also prioritize participants from underserved communities, including persons with disabilities and women from refugee and marginalized communities.
