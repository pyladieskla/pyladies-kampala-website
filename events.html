PyLadies Kampala Open Source Summit 2027

A one-day, hands-on Open Source Summit for women and other people interested in learning Python, exploring open source, and connecting with the wider tech community.

9 January 2027 · Kampala, Uganda

Register for the Summit

About the Summit

The Sixth Annual PyLadies Kampala Open Source Summit brings together beginners, developers, designers, and open-source enthusiasts for a day of learning and collaboration. The summit builds on our previous Open Source Summits and PyLadies community activities, with a focus on practical learning and helping participants take their first steps into open source.

From Our Previous Summits

Our summits bring the community together for talks, practical sessions, and conversations around open-source contribution — from CPython and plotnine contributions to Outreachy and other paid opportunities, and how designers and other non-developers can contribute to open source.

Each summit ends with networking, giving participants an opportunity to connect with speakers, mentors, and other members of the community.

What's Happening in 2027?

This year, we're putting even more focus on learning by doing.

Participants will spend the day working through practical sessions covering:

Python

Build a strong foundation in Python programming.

Web Development

Learn how Python can be used to build web applications.

Git & GitHub

Learn the basics of version control and collaborating on projects.

Open Source

Discover how open-source projects work and how to make your first contribution.

Career Opportunities

Learn about mentorship, open-source programs, and other ways to continue growing.

Learn With Mentors

You won't be learning alone.

Experienced developers and community members from PyLadies Kampala and the wider Python community will be available throughout the summit to help participants work through exercises, answer questions, and share their experiences.

Who Can Attend?

The summit is particularly welcoming to women and people who are underrepresented in technology.

Whether you're:

  • A student
  • New to programming
  • An early-career developer
  • Interested in Python
  • Curious about open source
  • Looking for your next learning opportunity

You're welcome.

No previous open-source experience is required.

Our Community

The Open Source Summit is part of the wider work PyLadies Kampala does throughout the year. We run regular meetups, workshops, and community activities covering Python, data science, web development, machine learning, open source, and career development.

In Arua, PyLadies Kampala has also taken its training beyond Kampala through a Python Web Development Bootcamp for refugees and members of the local community. The program gave participants several weeks of practical training in Python and web development, helping them build skills they can use to pursue further learning, employment, and opportunities in technology.
